Q.1 [10 Marks]

Find the rank of matrix A by reducing it to Normal Form [I_r 0; 0 0].

Step-by-Step Solution

Applying elementary row transformations R2 → R2 - 2R1, R3 → R3 - 3R1... Rank(A) = 3.

Is BTechX free to use?

Yes, 100% free. There are no paywalls, mandatory accounts, or commercial advertisements. Every syllabus module and question paper is open to all engineering students.

Are these notes aligned with university exams?

Yes. The units follow standard B.Tech university curriculum outlines (CGU, BPUT, VTU, AKTU) divided neatly into 5 discrete modules per subject with proofs, formulas, and diagrams.

Can I contribute my own lecture notes or past papers?

Absolutely. You can submit digital PDF notes and semester question papers to help juniors and peer students across universities.
